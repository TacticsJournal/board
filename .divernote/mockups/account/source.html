4 · Signed in with Pro
  • Sign in stays in the app. Settings › Account › Sign in opens a screen in the same sheet. The website's account panel is hidden in the app.
  • Sign in with Apple comes first. Apple requires it in any app that offers Google sign-in. Google opens in the system sign-in sheet, which Google allows; it doesn't open in the app's web view. Email codes work as they do on the web.
  • Everything still works without an account. Apple rejects apps that require sign-in for features that don't need an account.
  • Pro is an App Store subscription. It's bought with Apple Pay, with Restore purchases and the renewal terms Apple requires. Pro belongs to the Tactics Journal account, so someone who buys on iPhone also has Pro on the web.
  • Prices end in .99. App Store prices come from Apple's list of price points, so $155.88 a year isn't one of them. The mockup shows $155.99 to keep the same monthly price.
  • Manage subscription opens the App Store's subscription screen. The 14-day refund note is removed because Apple handles App Store refunds.
